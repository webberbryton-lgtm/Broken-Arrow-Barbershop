# Logos

Two marks, both Broken Arrow's own:

- **The BA monogram** — a B and an A cut from one block, with the arrow's point dropping out of the flared base.
- **The lockups** — the monogram above "BROKEN ARROW" in EB Garamond, with a descriptor spaced out between two rules: **BARBERSHOP** (the primary logo for the shop) and **GROOMING** (for products and packaging).

Files:

- `lockup-barbershop-black.svg` — ink `#161719`. Primary logo on `canvas` and light surfaces.
- `lockup-barbershop-offwhite.svg` — off-white `#f0ede7`. On black `band`s, dark green, photography and the dark theme.
- `lockup-barbershop-green.svg` — green `#445546`. Rarely: merch and print on off-white.
- `lockup-grooming-black.svg`, `-offwhite.svg`, `-green.svg` — the same three colours, for Broken Arrow Grooming.
- `ba-mark-black.svg` — ink `#161719`. The monogram alone, on light surfaces.
- `ba-mark-offwhite.svg` — off-white `#f0ede7`. The monogram on dark surfaces and photos.
- `ba-mark-green.svg` — green `#445546`. Merch, packaging, stamps.
- `ba-mark.png` — the original monogram as a transparent black PNG, for tools that can't take SVG.

The monogram and the name are traced from the supplied logo; the descriptor lines are set in EB Garamond to match. Replace with the original vector artwork when it's available.

When to use which:

- **Lockup** — where people first meet the brand: website header and footer, shop sign, business cards, the Square booking page, social profile banners.
- **Monogram alone** — small or repeated places: social avatar, favicon, jar lids, hats, stamps, watermarks on photos.
- The lockup's EB Garamond is the logo's own lettering; it isn't used for headlines or UI. Everything else stays in Inter Tight and Inter.
- New lockups (e.g. EDUCATION) follow the same build: same name, descriptor in EB Garamond Regular at the same size and spacing, rules the same length.

Rules: monogram at least 24px tall on screen and 12mm in print; lockup at least 160px wide on screen and 40mm in print. Clear space on every side equals the width of the B's counter. Never stretch, outline, rotate, recolour outside these three colours, add a drop shadow, re-type the wordmark in another font, or put either mark inside a badge or circle.
