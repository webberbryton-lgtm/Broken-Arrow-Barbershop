Longer hair, fewer visits

You can book any haircut as a Classic or a Signature. For mid-length and longer styles like a flow, warrior cut, modern mullet, etc., we recommend the Signature Haircut. Longer cuts need more detail work, and they don’t need cutting as often. Short haircuts grow out fast and need more frequent visits, which is exactly what the Classic Haircut is for.

Recommended for short cuts, fades, etc.

Included with every haircut

No charge for the wash.

Most shops nickel-and-dime you for every extra, and the wash is usually one of them. Ours is included with every haircut: we wash your hair, scrub your scalp with a scalp brush, and finish with a menthol scalp treatment and a hot towel. The scalp brush is one of our clients’ favorite parts.
