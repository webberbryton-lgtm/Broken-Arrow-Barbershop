Apply for a free haircut

We film transformation haircuts for our YouTube channel. If you’re picked, your cut is free and it goes on the channel.

We’re looking for big changes: thinning or receding hair, very long hair going short, or hair that hasn’t had a real cut in a long time. A clean-up of a cut you already like won’t make a video.

Already a client?

If you’re already a regular client, you probably won’t be selected. We’re looking for people who can create a big transformation on camera: long-to-short haircuts, major style changes, overgrown hair, and especially thinning or receding hairlines, including shorter hair that’s thinning.

If you’re just due for your normal maintenance haircut, please book with us as usual.
