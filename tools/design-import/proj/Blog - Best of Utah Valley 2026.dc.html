Broken Arrow Named a 2026 Best of Utah Valley Barbershop — Our Third Time Being Recognized

Shop news · September 26, 2026 · Broken Arrow Barbershop, Orem, Utah

Daily Herald · Readers’ Choice

Broken Arrow Barbershop has been named one of the top barbershops in the 2026 Daily Herald Best of Utah Valley Readers’ Choice Awards.

This is especially meaningful to us because it marks the third time Broken Arrow has been recognized in Best of Utah Valley.

Even more exciting, this recognition came only a few months after we opened our new Broken Arrow Barbershop location in Orem.

There are a lot of great barbers and barbershops throughout Utah County, and we’re grateful that our clients and community continue to support Broken Arrow.

01

Our third Best of Utah Valley recognition

Broken Arrow has changed and grown over the years, but the goal of the shop has remained simple: give people great haircuts, take the time to understand what they’re looking for, and create a barbershop they genuinely want to come back to.

Opening our new Orem location meant starting a new chapter for Broken Arrow. Receiving another Best of Utah Valley recognition after being in the new space for only a few months makes this one particularly special.

02

More than just a haircut

We believe a good men’s haircut should be designed around the person wearing it.

Hair type, growth patterns, face shape, hairline, lifestyle, and how someone actually styles their hair should all influence the final haircut.

That’s why our barbers take the time to understand what a client wants rather than simply giving everyone the same haircut.

Broken Arrow offers men’s haircuts, beard services, Signature Haircut appointments for larger transformations, and men’s hair replacement and hair systems for clients experiencing thinning hair or hair loss.

Whether you know exactly what haircut you want or need help figuring out what would work best for you, our goal is to help you leave with hair that actually works for you.

03

Thank you, Utah Valley

Best of Utah Valley is a community-voted recognition, which makes this award especially meaningful to us.

Our clients are the reason Broken Arrow continues to grow.

To everyone who has booked an appointment, left us a Google review, recommended the shop to a friend, supported one of our barbers, or voted for Broken Arrow: thank you.

We’re still building.

Our goal is to continue improving the shop, developing our barbers, and creating an exceptional men’s grooming experience here in Orem.

Receiving our third Best of Utah Valley recognition only a few months into our new location is an incredible way to begin this next chapter.
