About us · Payson to Orem

A barbershop first.

Broken Arrow is a small, locally owned men’s barbershop in Orem, Utah. We take the time to understand your hair before we cut it.

Who we are

Broken Arrow was founded by Bryton Webber, a licensed barber and licensed barber instructor. It started small, in Payson, and grew one client at a time.

Today we’re three barbers in Orem: Bryton, Connor and Elise. We cut fades, tapers, scissor cuts and longer styles, and we specialize in haircuts for thinning hair and non-surgical hair replacement.

01 / Payson

Where it started

Bryton opened Broken Arrow in Payson in 2022. The reputation came slowly: clients who came back, people who told their friends, and more than 240 Google reviews by the time the shop moved.

02 / The team

Trained here

Bryton taught hundreds of students at Paul Mitchell The School Provo, including Connor and Elise before they joined the shop. The same habits go into every cut: read the hair, make the call, explain it.

03 / Orem

A new chapter

The shop moved to a bigger space in Orem, and plenty of Payson clients still make the drive. Daily Herald readers have voted us Best Barbershop in Utah Valley in 2023, 2024 and 2026.

04 / Hair loss

More than haircuts

We help men with thinning hair and receding hairlines, first with the right haircut, and when that isn’t enough, with non-surgical hair systems that are color-matched and cut into your own hair.
