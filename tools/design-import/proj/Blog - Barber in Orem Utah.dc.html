04

Haircuts for thinning hair and receding hairlines

Choosing the right haircut matters more when your hair is thinning or your hairline is moving back.

A very high fade, lines that are too sharp, too much length, or the wrong styling can draw attention to the areas you’d rather play down. The right haircut works with the density, texture, hairline, and growth pattern you already have.

We’d rather help you find a style that suits the hair you actually have than give you the same cut as everyone else. Depending on how much hair you’ve lost and what you want, the options range from changing the haircut and how you style it to considering a hair system.

A haircut can change how thinning hair looks. It won’t stop or reverse hair loss.

Work with the hair you have, not against it.

Broken Arrow also offers non-surgical men’s hair replacement, also called hair systems. It’s for men with significant thinning, a receding hairline, or hair loss who want the look of fuller hair without surgery.
