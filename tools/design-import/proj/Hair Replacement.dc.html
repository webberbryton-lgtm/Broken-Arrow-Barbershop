Transplant or hair system?

We’re not against transplants.

For the right person, a transplant can work well. But Bryton has had several clients come to him after a transplant that didn’t give them the result they wanted. Most of them say they wish they’d tried a hair system first. It would have saved them a lot of money.

A transplant moves your own hair from the back and sides to the top. How well it works depends on how much donor hair you have and whether your hair loss keeps going. A hair system doesn’t depend on either, involves no surgery, and you can stop any time.

And if a system isn’t right for your hair, Bryton is just as happy to cut it. The consultation is free and there’s no pressure either way.

If a transplant is the right call

Bryton works directly with local doctors. If your hair is a good fit for a transplant, he can refer you to one here in Utah for about what people pay to fly to Turkey, without the travel or the risks of having surgery overseas.
