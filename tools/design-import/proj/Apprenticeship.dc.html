05 / Who should apply

Who Should Apply

The Broken Arrow Apprenticeship is for people who are serious about becoming exceptional barbers, not just getting through the required hours.

You should apply if you:

Are serious about building a long-term career in barbering.

Want hands-on experience in a real barbershop environment.

Are coachable and willing to take feedback.

Care about the details and want to continually improve your work.

Show up on time, work hard, and take responsibility.

Want to learn more than just how to cut hair, including consultation, client experience, content, branding, and building a clientele.

You probably shouldn’t apply if you:

Are looking for the easiest or fastest way to get licensed.

Regularly struggle with being on time or following through.

Aren’t comfortable receiving direct feedback on your work.

Expect clients or opportunities to be handed to you.

Aren’t willing to practice outside of the minimum requirements.

See barbering as something you’re only casually interested in trying.

We don’t expect apprentices to already be great barbers. We do expect them to be serious about becoming one.

06 / How it works

Utah’s new barbering permit, plainly

Utah now offers a barbering permit that takes 130 hours of approved training, instead of the 1,000+ hours of a traditional barber school. Here’s what that means.

  • What it lets you do

    Cut, clip and trim with shears and clippers, shampoo and style, and shave facial and neck hair. The classic barbering menu.

  • How you qualify

    130 hours of approved training, plus a passing score on the state barbering exam.

  • Where you train

    Inside Broken Arrow, a working shop in Orem, under a licensed barber instructor.

  • Where it leads

    A licensed start to your career, and a chair on our team if you earn it.
